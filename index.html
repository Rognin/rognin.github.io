About Me

Hi! I'm Artem, a game developer studying at Hochschule Darmstadt. My passion is gameplay programming, and for the past year I've been making games with Unity.

In my projects I focus on the user experience. If it's a game, I make sure it's fun, engaging and has a hook. If it's an app, I try to make it useful, convenient and elegant.

While I mostly work with Unity, I like to explore other engines and tools. You can learn more about my work in the Projects section.
